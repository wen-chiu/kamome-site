卡摸咩 Kamome 隱私權政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 28 日

卡摸咩 Kamome中文English

摘要

卡摸咩是一款把公路旅行變成旅程影片的 App。你的照片、旅程與影片都留在你的裝置上。卡摸咩沒有帳號系統、沒有自己的伺服器資料庫，也不使用任何廣告、分析或當機回報服務，不會追蹤你。

在裝置上使用的資料

  • 位置：只在你手動開始的旅程期間記錄路線；結束旅程即停止。記錄的路線存在你的裝置上。
  • 動作與健身：用來分辨開車與步行以節省電力，只在裝置上處理。
  • 照片：卡摸咩讀取照片的拍攝時間與地點，把照片放到路線上。照片不會被複製或上傳。你也可以只授權部分照片。
  • 儲存影片：只有在你點選「儲存」時，才會把完成的影片存到你的相簿。

哪些資料會離開你的裝置

為了提供功能，以下資料會送到第三方服務，且只有這些：

  • 地點名稱（Apple）：為了替旅程與地點命名，卡摸咩會把地點的座標送給 Apple 查詢地名。
  • 地圖與地形圖磚（OpenFreeMap、Amazon）：製作影片時，會從 OpenFreeMap 下載該區域的地圖圖磚，並從 Amazon（AWS）下載地形圖磚。這些主機會看到你的 IP 位址、圖磚座標、User-Agent 與請求時間；圖磚請求的順序會沿著路線進行。
  • 道路比對（Geoapify，經卡摸咩中繼）：對於從照片建立的旅程，卡摸咩會送出地點中心與照片拍攝位置（經過稀疏化並設有上限），以便沿真實道路繪製路線。這些請求先經過卡摸咩放在 Cloudflare 上的中繼服務，該中繼不保留轉送內容、只計算請求次數，也不轉送可識別你裝置的資料。依 Geoapify 的隱私政策，成功的請求最多保留 24 小時。由手機記錄的旅程不會送到道路比對服務。例外是合併兩趟旅程：兩段之間的空白會像照片旅程一樣對齊道路，因此會送出前一段的終點和下一段的起點。
  • 分享：完成的影片在你分享之前不會送到任何地方；你分享時，它會送往你選擇的去處。

你可以透過選擇要匯入的日期範圍或相簿，決定會送出哪些資料。「足跡」也會為找到的每趟旅程向 Apple 查詢名稱，因此會送出該旅程主要地點的座標。這只在你打開 App 裡的「足跡」後才會開始。

我們不做的事

  • 不收集個人資料到我們的伺服器，不建立帳號。
  • 不出售或出租資料，不投放廣告，不做跨 App 追蹤。
  • 不使用分析或當機回報的第三方服務。

資料保存與刪除

所有旅程與影片都存放在你的裝置上。你可以在 App 內刪除旅程，或刪除 App 以移除所有資料。你隨時可以在 iOS「設定」中撤回位置、照片與動作的存取權限。

兒童

卡摸咩並非針對 13 歲以下兒童設計，也不會刻意收集兒童的資料。

政策變更

若資料處理方式有所變更，我們會更新本頁與生效日期，並在 App 內的隱私說明中如實寫明。

Kamome Privacy Policy

Effective: October 28, 2026

Summary

Kamome turns road trips into recap films. Your photos, trips and films stay on your device. Kamome has no accounts and no server-side database of its own, uses no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services, and does not track you.

Data used on your device

  • Location: recorded only during a trip you start, and stopped the moment you end it. The recorded route stays on your device.
  • Motion & Fitness: used to tell driving from walking to save battery, processed on the device only.
  • Photos: Kamome reads where and when your photos were taken to place them on your route. Photos are never copied or uploaded. Limited (selected-photos) access works too.
  • Saving films: a finished film is saved to your photo library only when you tap Save.

What leaves your device

To provide its features, Kamome sends the following to third-party services, and nothing else:

  • Place names (Apple): to name trips and stops, Kamome sends a stop's coordinates to Apple to look up its name.
  • Map and terrain tiles (OpenFreeMap, Amazon): when rendering a film, Kamome downloads map tiles from OpenFreeMap and terrain tiles from Amazon (AWS) for the trip's area. These hosts see your IP address, the tile coordinates, a User-Agent string and the time of each request; the sequence of tile requests follows the route.
  • Road matching (Geoapify, via Kamome's relay): for trips built from photos, Kamome sends stop centres and photo positions (thinned and capped per leg) so the route follows real roads. These requests pass through Kamome's relay on Cloudflare, which keeps no record of what it forwards (it only counts requests) and forwards nothing that identifies your device. Geoapify's own privacy policy says successful requests are kept for no longer than 24 hours. Trips recorded by your phone are not sent to the routing service. One exception: when you merge two trips, the gap between them is matched to roads like a photo trip, so the end of one and the start of the next are sent.
  • Sharing: a finished film goes nowhere until you share it. When you do, it goes where you send it.

You decide what is sent by deciding what to import: a date range, or a single album. Footprints also asks Apple for the name of each journey it finds, which means sending the coordinates of that journey's main place. This begins only when you open Footprints in the app.

What we don't do

  • We do not collect personal data on our servers or create accounts.
  • We do not sell or rent data, show ads, or track you across apps.
  • We do not use third-party analytics or crash reporting.

Retention and deletion

All trips and films are stored on your device. You can delete a trip in the app, or delete the app to remove all of its data. You can revoke location, photos and motion access at any time in iOS Settings.

Children

Kamome is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If how Kamome handles data changes, we will update this page and its effective date, and say so in the app's privacy notice.
